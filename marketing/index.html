The problemGreat data.
Zero clarity.

Every coach knows the traditional stats — AVG, RBI, SO, ERA, etc. Some go deeper with sabermetrics OPS, C%, WHIP, and FIP. But after every game, GameChanger hands you a dashboard and downloadable spreadsheet and leaves the rest to you. Knowing the stats and knowing what to do about them are two very different things.

These metrics were built for professional baseball. At the youth level, coaches need signals — not just stats — that reflect where their players actually are in their development and what to focus on this week.

Dugout Signals surfaces those signals — built for the realities of coaching kids, not coaching professionals.
